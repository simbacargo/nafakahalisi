About us

Built for better food and stronger communities.

Nafaka Halisi is an Arusha mill connecting local farmers with families, businesses and institutions that need clean products.

Our story

Local quality you can trust.

Nafaka Halisi Tanzania is a fully registered Tanzanian food-processing company operating in a high-standard hygienic environment.

We work closely with northern Tanzania's farmers to source quality maize and wheat while helping them secure a dependable market. Modern equipment allows us to serve families, food vendors and bakeries.
